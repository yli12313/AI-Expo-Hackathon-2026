# Duty Line — Demo Script

5-minute walkthrough for judges, investors, and policymakers.
Each module builds to a deliverable. Show the time saved.

---

## TDY Travel (2 min) — ends with a filled DD 1610

**Prompt 1:**
> What's the per diem rate for Columbus, Georgia?

*Shows: instant rate lookup from cached GSA data — no manual table searching.*

**Prompt 2:**
> I need to send SPC Rivera there for 5 days starting July 10. She's driving her POV from Fort Liberty. What's the total cost?

*Shows: full cost breakdown — lodging, meals (75% travel day rule applied), mileage. All JTR-compliant math done in seconds.*

**Prompt 3:**
> Generate the DD 1610 for this trip.

*Shows: filled PDF ready for download. What normally takes 30+ minutes of form hunting and manual entry — done in under 60 seconds across three messages.*

**Time saved: 2-4 hours → 60 seconds**

## Leave / HR (1.5 min) — ends with a filled DA 31

**Prompt 1:**

*Shows: regulation lookup with citation — 2.5 days/month = 45 days accrued.*

**Prompt 2:**
> I need 10 days ordinary leave starting June 15 to visit family in Texas. Am I eligible and can you fill the DA 31?

*Shows: eligibility check + filled DA 31 PDF with soldier profile auto-populated. One message replaces the entire leave request workflow.*

**Time saved: 1-2 hours → 30 seconds**
